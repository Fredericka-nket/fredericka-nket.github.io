Early Breast Cancer Detection

  • 96.5%accuracy on 114 held-out patients
  • 92.9%recall: 39 of 42 malignant tumours caught
  • 0.996AUC (area under the ROC curve)
  • 30 to 21features after hill-climbing, with identical scores
What it is
A machine-learning study of whether tumour measurements can support early breast cancer detection. The team of four used the Wisconsin Diagnostic Breast Cancer (WDBC) dataset, which holds 569 patients and 30 numeric features, and compared two approaches: a logistic regression classifier, and hill-climbing feature selection that searches for a smaller set of inputs. A random forest scored candidate feature sets inside the search, and logistic regression was the final model.
My role
I contributed to the hill-climbing feature-selection component and wrote the group's technical report.
What the results show
Logistic regression caught 39 of 42 malignant tumours with one false alarm. The 21-feature subset gave identical scores. Across eight further data splits the subset matched the full model on recall and AUC and was 0.3 percentage points lower on accuracy. The search shortened the model but did not beat random subsets of the same size, and the subset it chose varied from 13 to 30 features between splits. The report states these limits and suggests lowering the decision threshold to reduce missed cancers.

Fitness Tracker

What it is
A console application that converts a walking distance in kilometres into steps (1,312 steps per kilometre) and describes the result in plain English, such as "6 thousands, 5 hundreds and 6 tens". It also tracks a personal step goal, keeps a timestamped activity log and prints a session summary.
My role
I did all of it: the design of three classes with separate responsibilities (Distance, StepStats and UserInterfaceMain), the code, the test tables and the written report.
Challenge
The remaining-steps message was wrong for large distances because the total was calculated in one place and the displayed breakdown in another. Rounding also happened after the breakdown in early versions. I fixed both by rounding once, immediately after the conversion, and rebuilding the total from the same values the user sees.
Skills developed
Debugging from a symptom back to its cause, validating every kind of bad input (letters, symbols, zero, negative and very large numbers), and recording tests in a table of inputs, expected output and result.
